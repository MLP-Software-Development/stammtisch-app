# Stammtisch

Small groups that meet regularly plan their events, answer whether they come, share a cash box, photos and documents.

## Groups and people

**Group**:
A set of people who meet regularly and share events, money and files, e.g. one Stammtisch or one club. A person can
belong to many groups.
_Avoid_: Team, club, tenant, community

**Member**:
A person who belongs to a group.
_Avoid_: User (for the group-scoped person), player, participant

**Admin**:
A member allowed to manage the group: its events, money, documents and members.
_Avoid_: Owner, moderator, organiser

**Invite link**:
A link that lets a person join a group as a member; an admin can replace it to stop it working.
_Avoid_: Invitation code

**Stewardship**:
A topic a group hands to one member to take care of, named freely by the group (e.g. "Games", "Trips"), and handed
on to another member over time.
_Avoid_: Role, duty, task, "Wart"

**Steward**:
The member who currently holds a stewardship.
_Avoid_: Owner, lead, responsible

## Events and answers

**Event**:
One gathering of a group at a date and time.
_Avoid_: Appointment, meeting, date, termin

**Series**:
A rule that creates events on a fixed rhythm (e.g. every 4th week, every 2nd Friday of the month); each event in it
can still be moved or cancelled on its own.
_Avoid_: Recurrence, recurring event

**RSVP**:
A member's answer to an event: Yes, No, or Pending while they have not answered. A Yes can carry one guest and a
note.
_Avoid_: Response, attendance, registration, Maybe

**Guest**:
One extra person a member brings along with their Yes (a "+1"); counted in the headcount, not a member.
_Avoid_: Plus-one, companion

**RSVP deadline**:
The optional moment after which an answer to an event counts as late.
_Avoid_: Cutoff, closing date

**Late change**:
An RSVP changed after the RSVP deadline; allowed unless the event is binding, and always visible as late.
_Avoid_: Late cancellation

**Binding event**:
An event whose Yes, once the RSVP deadline has passed, the member can no longer turn into a No; they can only mark
that they cannot attend, and stay counted.
_Avoid_: Fixed event, mandatory event

**Capacity**:
The optional maximum number of people, members and guests together, an event takes.
_Avoid_: Limit, max attendees

**Proposal**:
Something the group could do, with or without a date, added by any member; it asks for no RSVP and sends no
reminders until an admin promotes it to an event.
_Avoid_: Idea, tip, suggestion

**Interested**:
A member's mark on a proposal that they would like to go; never counts as a Yes.
_Avoid_: Like, vote, maybe

**Draft**:
An event only admins can see, not yet published, so nobody is notified about it.
_Avoid_: Unpublished event, hidden event

**Calendar feed**:
A member's personal subscription link that puts their groups' events into their own calendar app.
_Avoid_: Calendar sync, ICS export

## Money

**Cash ledger**:
The record of a group's shared money, mirroring the group's bank account.
_Avoid_: Kasse, cash box, wallet, account

**Dues**:
The recurring amount each member owes the group's cash ledger.
_Avoid_: Fee, contribution, membership fee

## Files

**Album**:
The photos of one event, visible either to that event's attendees only or to all members of the group.
_Avoid_: Gallery, photo folder

**Document**:
A file or text page that belongs to a group, such as its rules.
_Avoid_: Attachment (for group documents), file, page

## Product

**Feature request**:
A wish for the product itself, sent by anyone, kept as a public issue without personal data.
_Avoid_: Idea, proposal (a proposal is about what the group does, not about the product)
